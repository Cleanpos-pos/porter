A Winchester Christmas, Done Properly

Christmas in Winchester has a charm of its own: lights along the High Street, the cathedral close in the cold, and a table booked somewhere warm for afterwards. Porterhouse Steakhouse sits right in the middle of it at 24 Jewry Street, a short walk from it all.

Our Christmas Party Menu 2026 is built for work parties, family get-togethers and friends who only manage to sit down together once a year. It keeps to what we do best — four char-grilled steak cuts from our steakhouse kitchen — alongside a festive turkey roulade, grilled salmon and a vegetarian pasta, so there is something for everyone at the table.

One price per person, a choice at every course, and everything pre-ordered so your party is looked after from the moment you sit down.
